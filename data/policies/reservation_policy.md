# Reservation Policy & Late Arrival Rules

## 1. Advance Booking Guidelines
- Reservations can be booked up to 30 days in advance for parties of 1 to 12 guests.
- Special requests (high chair, anniversary, quiet corner, wheelchair access) must be declared at booking time.

## 2. Table Holding Window
- Tables designated for confirmed reservations are reserved 30 minutes prior to the scheduled reservation time to prevent conflicting walk-in allocations.
- A grace period of 15 minutes is granted past the reservation timestamp.
- If a party fails to arrive within the 15-minute grace period without prior notification, their reservation status is automatically converted to `NO_SHOW` or released, and the table returns to the `AVAILABLE` pool for waiting walk-ins.

## 3. Cancellation Policy
- Cancellations made at least 2 hours before the scheduled time incur zero penalty.
- Same-day cancellations within 30 minutes of reservation may be logged for customer profile auditing.
