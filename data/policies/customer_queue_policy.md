# Customer Queue & Priority Policy

## 1. Queue Ordering & Waiting Times
- Walk-in guests join the live customer queue upon arrival with a generated queue ID.
- Base queue discipline is First-Come, First-Served (FCFS) modulated by priority multipliers:
  - Standard Walk-in: Priority Level 1 (Weight 1.0)
  - Registered Loyalty / VIP Guest: Priority Level 2 (Weight 1.3)
  - Pre-Confirmed Delayed Reservation: Priority Level 3 (Weight 1.6)
  - High-Priority Emergency / Special Assistance: Priority Level 4 (Weight 2.0)

## 2. Queue Abandonment & Renege Limits
- Customer patience limits vary by party size and restaurant occupancy:
  - Parties of 1-2: 25 minutes average tolerance before reneging.
  - Parties of 3-4: 35 minutes average tolerance.
  - Parties of 5+: 45 minutes average tolerance.
- Live wait estimates must be refreshed continuously to minimize reneging.

## 3. Large Group & Split Party Policy
- Parties of 6 or more require combined tables or designated banquet tables (e.g., T11, T12).
- If no single large table is available within 20 minutes, customers may opt for adjacent paired tables under the same designated waiter.

## 4. Accessibility & Special Seating Policy
- Guests requiring wheelchair accessibility must be assigned Ground Floor, Wide Aisle tables (T01-T04) without step transitions.
- High-chair requests require tables with clear peripheral clearance away from primary kitchen traffic corridors.
